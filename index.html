Company Overview

Hindustan Products and Control Systems is a leading provider of cutting-edge industrial automation solutions. Established in 2024, we specialize in factory automation, robotics, and PLC-based control systems, helping businesses optimize efficiency, reduce downtime, and improve productivity. Our solutions cater to diverse industries, including manufacturing and energy.

Our Expertise:

  • Factory Automation & Industrial Robotics – Seamless automation for smart factories
  • PLC Suppliers & Programming – Mitsubishi, Omron, Siemens, Schneider, IDEC, Fuji Electric
  • Chain Conveyor & Machine Modifications – Optimizing performance and durability
  • Special Purpose Machines (SPM) – Custom-built machines for industrial needs
  • Power Quality Solutions – SVG, AHF, Ultra AHF & Ultra SVG for improved power reliability and efficiency
  • SCADA Systems – Mitsubishi, Delta, Siemens, Schneider, Omron, Pro-face for real-time monitoring, data acquisition, and centralized process control
